# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** December 2024  
**Company:** LIFESPROUT Care  
**App:** BillSprout - Smart ERP & Billing System

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Name, email address, phone number
- Business details (store name, address, GST number)
- Payment information (processed securely via Razorpay)
- Device information and usage analytics

### Business Data
- Product inventory and pricing
- Sales transactions and billing records
- Customer purchase history
- Employee access logs

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide ERP and billing services
- Process payments securely
- Generate business reports and analytics
- Provide customer support
- Send important service notifications
- Comply with legal requirements

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share data with:
- **Payment Processors:** Razorpay for secure payment processing
- **Cloud Storage:** Supabase for secure data storage
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
- Secure authentication and authorization
- Regular security audits and updates
- Data backup and recovery systems

## 5. Data Retention

- Transaction data: 7 years (as per GST compliance)
- User accounts: Until account deletion requested
- Analytics data: 2 years maximum
- Backup data: 90 days retention

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Export your business data
- Opt-out of non-essential communications

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Maintain user sessions
- Remember user preferences
- Analyze app usage patterns
- Improve user experience

## 8. Third-Party Services

### Razorpay (Payment Processing)
- Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy/
- Data processed: Payment details, transaction records

### Supabase (Database & Authentication)
- Privacy Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy
- Data processed: All app data and user information

## 9. Children's Privacy

BillSprout is intended for business use only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in servers located in different countries. We ensure adequate protection through:
- Standard contractual clauses
- Data processing agreements
- Regular compliance audits

## 11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes via:
- In-app notifications
- Email announcements
- Website updates
