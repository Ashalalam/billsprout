# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** December 2024  
**Company:** LIFESPROUT Care  
**App:** BillSprout - Smart ERP & Billing System

## 1. Subscription Cancellation

### How to Cancel
- **Self-Service:** Cancel via app settings
- **Email Request:** Send to support@billsprout.online
- **WhatsApp:** Message +44 7747 571513
- **No cancellation fees** - Cancel anytime

### Cancellation Processing
- **Immediate Effect:** Service continues until end of paid period
- **Data Access:** Full access until subscription expires
- **Grace Period:** 30 days post-expiry for data export
- **Confirmation:** Email confirmation sent within 24 hours

## 2. Refund Policy

### Eligible Refunds
- **Technical Issues:** Service unavailable for 48+ hours
- **Billing Errors:** Duplicate charges or incorrect amounts
- **Service Not Delivered:** Features promised but not available
- **Cancellation within 7 days:** Full refund for annual plans

### Non-Refundable Items
- **Partial Months:** No refund for unused portions of monthly plans
- **Change of Mind:** After 7-day refund period
- **User Error:** Incorrect usage or configuration
- **Third-Party Costs:** Payment gateway fees and taxes

## 3. Refund Process

### Request Procedure
1. **Email:** refunds@billsprout.online
2. **Subject:** Include order ID and reason
3. **Documentation:** Provide relevant details
4. **Response Time:** 48 hours for acknowledgment

### Processing Timeline
- **Review Period:** 5-7 business days
- **Approval Notification:** Email confirmation
- **Refund Processing:** 7-10 business days
- **Bank Credit:** 3-5 additional business days

### Required Information
- Account email address
- Order/transaction ID
- Date of purchase
- Reason for refund request
- Supporting documentation (if applicable)

## 4. Refund Methods

### Payment Method
- **Credit/Debit Card:** Refunded to original card
- **Net Banking:** Refunded to source account
- **UPI:** Refunded to original UPI ID
- **Wallet:** Refunded to original wallet

### Currency and Fees
- **Currency:** Indian Rupees (INR) only
- **Gateway Fees:** Non-refundable (2-3% of transaction)
- **International Cards:** Additional forex charges may apply
- **Processing Fees:** Deducted from refund amount

## 5. Service-Specific Refund Rules

### Annual Subscriptions
- **7-Day Grace:** Full refund within 7 days
- **Pro-rated Refund:** After 7 days, calculated monthly
- **Minimum Charge:** First month non-refundable after 7 days
- **Technical Issues:** Full refund for service unavailability

### Monthly Subscriptions
- **No Partial Refunds:** Monthly fees non-refundable
- **Current Month:** Service continues until period end
- **Next Billing:** Automatically cancelled
- **Technical Issues:** Service extension instead of refund

### Add-on Services
- **Unused Credits:** Refundable within 30 days
- **Premium Features:** Pro-rated refund available
- **One-time Purchases:** 7-day refund window
- **Custom Development:** Non-refundable after work begins

## 13. FAQs

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**  
A: Yes, you can cancel anytime without any cancellation fees.

**Q: Will I get a refund for the remaining days?**  
A: For annual plans within 7 days - yes. For monthly plans - no partial refunds.

**Q: How long does refund processing take?**  
A: 7-10 business days after approval, plus 3-5 days for bank credit.

**Q: What if my payment failed but money was debited?**  
A: Auto-refund within 24 hours. Contact support if not received.

**Q: Can I get refund for add-on services?**  
A: Yes, within 30 days if unused or service not delivered.
